HOW TO USE THE RIDE WORKFLOW

1. Open /admin.html and enter the PIN.
2. Tap Load / Refresh Rides.
3. Move a ride through:
   New > Acknowledged > En route > Arrived > Passenger picked up > Completed
4. Completed and canceled rides move into Ride History.
5. When Start Driving is tapped, allow location access and keep the admin page open.
6. The rider uses the private tracking link created after their request.

Note: iPhone browsers may pause location updates when the browser is closed or the phone is locked for a while. The dashboard must remain open for dependable live updates.
